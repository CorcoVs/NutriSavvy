What do NOVA and Nutri-Score mean?

NOVA groups

  • 1 — Unprocessed or minimally processed
  • 2 — Processed culinary ingredients (oils, sugar, salt)
  • 3 — Processed foods
  • 4 — Ultra-processed foods
